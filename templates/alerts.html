Frequently Asked Questions

Is BusCompare price alert really free? +

Yes! The Free plan is 100% free forever. You can set 1 route alert and receive email notifications at no cost. The Pro plan (₹99/month) gives you unlimited routes and WhatsApp alerts.

How fast will I get notified when prices drop? +

Free plan users get daily email notifications. Pro plan users get instant alerts within 5 minutes of a price drop via WhatsApp and Email.

What platforms do you monitor for price alerts? +

We monitor RedBus, AbhiBus, MakeMyTrip, Yatra, and direct operator bookings. When any platform drops its price, you get notified with the direct booking link.

Can I cancel my price alert subscription? +

Absolutely. You can unsubscribe from alerts at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link in any email we send. No questions asked.

Will you spam my WhatsApp or email? +

We respect your privacy. We only send alerts when prices actually drop. No promotional spam, no third-party sharing. Average user gets 2-3 alerts per week.
