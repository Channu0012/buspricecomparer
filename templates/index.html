Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

BusCompare India collects information you provide directly: email addresses, phone numbers (optional), route preferences for price alerts. We also collect anonymized usage data (search queries, pages viewed) to improve our platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your contact information solely to send price drop alerts you've subscribed to, service updates, and promotional offers (only with your consent). We never sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Affiliate Links

BusCompare is an affiliate platform. When you click "Book Now," we redirect you to partner platforms (RedBus, AbhiBus, MakeMyTrip) and may earn a commission. This does not affect the price you pay.

4. Cookies

We use localStorage to remember your theme preference and language selection. No third-party tracking cookies are used without your consent.

5. Data Security

All data is stored securely. Price alert subscriptions are encrypted in transit. You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing privacy@buscompare.in.

6. Contact Us

For privacy concerns, email: privacy@buscompare.in

Terms of Service

Last updated: July 2026

1. Acceptance

By using BusCompare India, you agree to these terms. BusCompare is a price comparison and information platform — we do not sell or book tickets directly.

2. Price Accuracy

Prices shown are indicative and fetched from partner platforms. Actual prices may vary based on seat availability, date of booking, and demand. Always verify prices on the final booking platform before payment.

3. Price Lock Feature

The ₹39 Price Lock fee is non-refundable. It guarantees the displayed fare for 6 hours. If the operator changes prices, BusCompare will honour the locked price via affiliate credit.

4. VIP Subscription

VIP BusPass subscriptions auto-renew monthly (₹99) or annually (₹299). You may cancel anytime from your account. Refunds are issued pro-rata for annual plans cancelled within 30 days.

5. Limitation of Liability

BusCompare is not liable for booking failures, cancellations, or changes made by third-party operators. All disputes regarding tickets must be resolved with the respective booking platform.

6. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Any disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of Bangalore, Karnataka courts.
