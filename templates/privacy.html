🔒 Security & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 27, 2026 | Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023

1. Overview

At BusCompare India, your privacy and data security are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal personal information strictly necessary to provide price alert notifications and review submissions:

  • Email Address: Collected when you subscribe to price alerts.
  • WhatsApp Number (Optional): Collected only if you explicitly request WhatsApp notifications for price drops.
  • Reviews & Ratings: Display name, city, rating, and feedback submitted voluntarily.
  • Telemetry & Analytics: Anonymous click data (route, platform selected) to improve comparison accuracy.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used for the following operational purposes:

  • Sending price drop notifications via Email or WhatsApp.
  • Publishing verified user reviews for bus operators.
  • Preventing fraud, bot traffic, and automated scraping attacks.

We NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertisers.

4. Data Encryption & Security

We implement enterprise-grade security protocols to prevent unauthorized access, hacking, or data breaches:

  • HTTPS/TLS Encryption: All data in transit is encrypted using 256-bit SSL encryption.
  • Security Headers & Protection: Protected by automated rate limiting, XSS filtering, CSP policies, and anti-CSRF protection.
  • Restricted Access: Administrative analytics are protected by multi-factor passkeys and session tokens.

5. Third-Party Links & Affiliate Disclosure

BusCompare India is a comparison engine. When you click "Book Now", you are redirected to official partner booking portals (such as RedBus, AbhiBus, MakeMyTrip). We do not collect or store your payment card details, bank account numbers, or UPI credentials. All transactions occur directly on partner websites.

6. Your Rights & Unsubscribe

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to request deletion of your stored email or WhatsApp number at any time. You can instantly unsubscribe from alerts by emailing privacy@buscompare.in or clicking the unsubscribe link in any notification.
